# JellyNet design

## Cinema marquee

Household viewers should be able to choose a title from a sofa or while holding a phone. The home starts with edge-to-edge library artwork. A graduated wine-red scrim keeps the title and actions readable, and the first shelf begins where the artwork fades. Phone layouts keep the featured title compact so the shelf appears in the first viewport.

- Use wine-red surfaces, cream text, and amber for primary actions, current navigation, and keyboard focus.
- Keep metadata subordinate to the title. Use normal text links for secondary actions and give poster shelves enough space to scan.
- Keep the navigation drawer flat and grouped by task. Use the same focus treatment for cards, links, and form controls.
- Use artwork supplied by the Jellyfin library. Give the layout a usable fallback when artwork is absent. Keep the featured banner static.
